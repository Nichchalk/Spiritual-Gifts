# Spiritual Gifts Quiz

A web application that helps users discover and understand their spiritual gifts through an interactive quiz based on Ron Kingham's framework. Get personalized insights with a score dashboard and export your results.

## About

This project is built on Ron Kingham's spiritual gifts assessment methodology. Whether you're exploring your faith, looking for personal growth, or seeking to understand how you can best contribute to your community, this quiz provides a comprehensive framework for identifying your unique spiritual gifts.

## Features

✨ **Interactive Quiz**
- Comprehensive assessment based on Ron Kingham's spiritual gifts framework
- User-friendly interface for smooth quiz experience
- Immediate feedback after completion

📊 **Personal Score Dashboard**
- Visual presentation of your spiritual gifts
- Personalized insights for each gift
- Easy-to-understand scoring breakdown

📥 **Export Functionality**
- Download your results as a CSV file
- Keep a personal record of your assessment
- Share your results with others

## Usage

### Taking the Quiz

1. Visit the application homepage
2. Click **"Start Quiz"** to begin
3. Answer each question thoughtfully
4. Submit your responses to generate your results

### Viewing Your Results

1. After completing the quiz, you'll see your **Score Dashboard**
2. Review your top spiritual gifts and their descriptions
3. Explore the insights provided for each gift

### Downloading Your Results

1. On your results dashboard, click the **"Download CSV"** button
2. Your results will be saved as a CSV file with the following information:
   - Quiz completion date
   - Individual gift scores
   - Gift rankings
   - Personal insights
